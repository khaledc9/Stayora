Every Moment, Crafted

Signature Experiences

From the first cup of coffee to the last sip of wine, each experience is an invitation to be fully present.

Mornings

Farm-to-Table Breakfast

Begin your day with a breakfast sourced entirely from our gardens and neighboring farms. Fresh-baked croissants, seasonal fruit, estate-roasted coffee, and eggs from our heritage-breed hens — served on the terrace as the valley wakes.

☀ Served daily 7:00 – 10:30 AM
🌱 Organic, garden-sourced ingredients
☕ Estate-roasted single-origin coffee
Grounds

Garden Walks & Foraging

Wander twelve acres of curated gardens and preserved forest with our head gardener. Learn to identify wild herbs, seasonal blossoms, and edible plants. The walk ends at the greenhouse with a tasting of house-made preserves and herbal infusions.

🌿 Guided walks twice weekly
🌵 Foraging workshop included
🍅 Tasting at the greenhouse
Culture

Local Village Tours

Visit the nearby village with a local guide who knows every artisan, farmer, and storyteller. Browse the morning market, watch craftspeople at work, and share tea with a family whose roots here go back generations. A genuine encounter with the culture that surrounds Stayora.

☮ Half-day guided tour
🏫 Artisan workshop visits
☕ Traditional tea ceremony
Adventure

Mountain Hiking Trails

Lace up and step onto trails that begin at our doorstep. From gentle forest loops to summit hikes with panoramic valley views, our trail map offers routes for every level. Guided hikes include a packed picnic and a naturalist's perspective on the local ecosystem.

⛰ Trails for all skill levels
🍰 Gourmet picnic lunch included
👀 Naturalist-guided option
Wellness

Spa & Wellness Sanctuary

Our spa draws from local traditions — forest-inspired aromatherapy, hot stone massage using river-smoothed basalt, and herbal compresses made from garden-grown botanicals. Each treatment is preceded by a consultation to tailor the experience to your needs.

☮ Six treatment rooms
🌿 Botanical, garden-sourced products
☀ Outdoor relaxation terrace
Evenings

Sunset Dinner Series

As the light softens, our chef prepares a multi-course tasting menu at a private table on the ridge. Each course is paired with wines from regional vineyards and timed to the sunset. It's the kind of evening that lingers long after the last course.

☀ Ridge-top private dining
🍷 Sommelier wine pairing
🍼 Seasonal tasting menu
