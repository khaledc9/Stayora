Mountain Suite

★★★★★ 4.9 (128 reviews) ● 3 Guests ● 1 King + Sofa Bed ● 60 m²

Perched on the hillside with floor-to-ceiling windows framing the valley below, the Mountain Suite is our most sought-after accommodation. Wake to layers of mist rolling across the peaks, and end your day beside the fireplace as the last light fades.

The suite features a king-sized bed dressed in organic linens, a separate sitting area with a sofa bed, and a spa-inspired bathroom with a deep soaking tub positioned to capture the view. Natural materials — warm oak, local stone, and brushed brass — create a sense of calm that complements rather than competes with the landscape outside.
