Our History

From Family Estate to Sanctuary

Stayora began as a family retreat — a place where three generations would gather each summer to escape the city and reconnect with the land. What started as a single farmhouse on twelve acres of forest has, over two decades, become a carefully expanded resort that retains the intimacy of a private home.

Every building was designed to sit within the landscape rather than dominate it. Local materials, local craftspeople, and local traditions shaped every corner. Today, Stayora welcomes guests from around the world while remaining deeply rooted in this place.

Milestones

Our Journey

2004

The Family Estate

The original farmhouse is purchased as a summer retreat for the family. Twelve acres of forest, a stream, and a view of the valley below.

2011

First Guest Rooms

The estate's barn and outbuildings are sensitively converted into five guest rooms, welcoming the first visitors with the family's signature breakfast.

2016

The Garden & Spa

Organic gardens are established and the spa sanctuary opens, built from local stone and staffed by therapists trained in traditional techniques.

2020

Full Resort Expansion

Eighteen rooms and suites, a pool, the ridge dining terrace, and a full curatorial team. Stayora becomes the retreat it is today.

2026

Looking Forward

New sustainability initiatives, an expanded wellness program, and partnerships with local artisans — all while preserving the intimacy that defines Stayora.

Beyond Our Gates

Rooted in Local Culture

The village beyond our gates has been here for centuries — its markets, artisans, and traditions predate the resort by generations. We are guests in this community, and we take that responsibility seriously.

We source from local farmers, employ local craftspeople, and direct our guests to experiences that benefit the community directly. Our village tours are led by residents, not guides bussed in from the city. Every visit to Stayora supports the ecosystem of people who call this valley home.
